Unit 1: Quantum Mechanics
Introduction to Quantum Physics
Quantum mechanics is a fundamental theory in physics that provides a description of the physical properties of nature at the scale of atoms and subatomic particles. It differs from classical physics primarily at the quantum realm of atomic and subatomic length scales.

Key Concepts
Wave-Particle Duality: Particles exhibit both wave-like and particle-like properties.
Uncertainty Principle: You cannot simultaneously know the exact position and momentum of a particle.
Quantization of Energy: Energy is not continuous but comes in discrete packets called quanta.
"If you think you understand quantum mechanics, you don't understand quantum mechanics." — Richard Feynman
